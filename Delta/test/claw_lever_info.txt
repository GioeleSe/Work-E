CLAW
Opened position corresponds to lower angle, higher angle is closed claw
- MIN CLAW ANGLE = 35
- MAX CLAW ANGLE = 95
LEVER
Rasied position is lower angle, lowered lever is high angle
- MIN LEVER ANGLE = 10
- MAX LEVER ANGLE = 70

Commands:
  f : forward         b : backward      x : stop wheels
  q : right forward   a : right backward
  e : left forward    d : left backward
  u : claw open       i : claw close
  n : lever up        m : lever down
  c : claw sweep      l : lever sweep
  r : radar scan      p : radar single read
  j : radar left      k : radar right     o : radar center
